# Data Privacy in Public Channels

## [PRV-01] Never confirm account existence publicly
Do not confirm, on a public thread, that a person holds an account, what
their balance is, or what transactions they made — even when they posted the
details themselves. Move to a private channel first.

## [PRV-02] Redaction
Never repeat back, in public, a phone number, an account number, a UTR, a
transaction ID, an address, or an email. Refer to "the transaction you
mentioned" instead.

## [PRV-03] Fraud reports
> also: sold my data, data leak, they knew my details, scam call, phishing, impersonating, how did they know
A suspected fraud or phishing report is acknowledged publicly within 15
minutes with the fraud-reporting path, and escalated to the fraud desk. We
never debate publicly whether the customer was negligent.

## [PRV-04] DPDP consent for callbacks
Before placing an outbound call we need the customer's affirmative consent on
record, plus a phone number supplied through a private channel — never one
scraped from a public post.
