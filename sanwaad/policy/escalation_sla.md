# Escalation Matrix and Service Levels

## [ESC-01] Public acknowledgement SLA
Every public complaint receives a first response within **60 minutes**
during 08:00–22:00 IST, and within 12 hours outside those windows.

## [ESC-02] When to move to voice
Offer a callback when any holds: the amount in dispute exceeds ₹2,000; the
complaint has been open more than 48 hours; the customer has posted more than
twice about the same issue; the category is account freeze or fraud; or the
customer explicitly asks to speak to someone.

## [ESC-03] Callback commitment
A callback offered publicly must be attempted within **4 working hours**. An
unfulfilled public callback promise is treated as a Severity-1 process breach.

## [ESC-04] Nodal officer
Unresolved after 30 days, the customer is entitled to the Nodal Officer, and
after that the RBI Ombudsman. We provide both proactively at day 30 rather
than waiting to be asked.

## [ESC-05] Regulatory-risk keywords
> also: ombudsman, consumer court, legal notice, police, FIR, cyber cell, media, journalist, lawyer, sue
Any complaint mentioning the RBI Ombudsman, a consumer court, a police
report, media, or a legal notice is routed to the compliance queue and is
never auto-answered, regardless of computed severity.

## [ESC-06] Repeat contact
A customer contacting us for the third time on one issue skips the standard
queue and goes directly to a senior agent callback.
