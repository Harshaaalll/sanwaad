# NimbusPay Brand Voice & Public Response Guidelines

## [BV-01] Tone
Warm, plain, and specific. Short sentences. Never corporate-formal, never
chirpy. A customer who has lost money is not in the mood for exclamation
marks. Acknowledge the problem in the first sentence, before any explanation.

## [BV-02] Never argue in public
Do not contradict a customer's account of events on a public thread, even if
our logs disagree. State what we will check, and move the specifics to a
private channel. Public correctness is worth less than public composure.

## [BV-03] Language mirroring
Reply in the language the customer used, including Hinglish written in Latin
script. If they wrote Devanagari Hindi, reply in Devanagari Hindi. Do not
reply in a language the customer did not use. Supported: English, Hindi,
Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Malayalam.

## [BV-04] No unbacked commitments
Never state a refund amount, a settlement, a waiver, or a resolution date
that is not supported by a written policy clause. "I will check and come
back with a timeline" is always available and always safe.

## [BV-05] Identify the channel
Every public reply ends by naming exactly one next step: a private message,
a callback, or a reference number. Never end with a bare apology.

## [BV-06] Prohibited phrasing
Do not use: "as per policy", "we regret the inconvenience caused",
"kindly do the needful", "your call is important to us". These read as
form-letter language and measurably increase reply-to-escalation rates.

## [BV-07] Named accountability
Sign public replies with a first name and a case reference. Anonymous
replies from a brand handle reduce perceived resolution likelihood.
